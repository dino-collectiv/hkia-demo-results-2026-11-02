Data issues

Gaps in the airport's map data behind these answers. Each one was checked directly against the data.

Routes go through a closed walkway

In O2 the agent was told that the walkway between LOEWE and Fortnum & Mason on Level 6 is closed. The shortest way from the passenger's spot to Gates 7, 8 and 9 is 375 m, and it runs through that walkway.

The expected longer way round on the same floor, 478 m past %Arabica, the Bank of China ATM and Gate 5, is in the map data.

Seen in: O2.

The airport train is recorded as a walk

Gates 201–230 are in the Midfield Concourse, which passengers reach from Terminal 1 on a short driverless train. In the map data that train ride is stored as an ordinary walkway, about 1,000–1,800 m long. So directions say “Turn left, 1,069 m” where they should say “take the train”, and the travel time counts the ride as if you walked it. The links to SkyPier and Terminal 2 are stored the same way.

Seen in: P2, P5, O5 — any route between Terminal 1 and the 200-series gates.

Gates 221–226 are missing

has its own entryonly on the sign “Gates 211–220”not in the data

Gates 201–219 and 227–230 each have their own entry in the map data. Gate 220 appears only as part of the overhead sign “Gates 211–220”. Gates 221–226 aren't in the data at all, so the agent can only say it can't find them.

Seen in: O5 — the first run asked about Gate 223; this run uses Gate 228.

Each gate is stored twice, on two floors

Gate 5 is stored once on Level 6, where departing passengers board, and again on Level 5, where arriving passengers leave the plane. The Level 5 copy is only a marker on the map, and nothing in the data says it is the arrival side, so the agent has to be told this separately in its description of the airport.

Seen in: P4 and P5 (arriving passengers), O1 (the reply mentions the Level 5 marker).

No step-free way up in some places

From the Level 6 departures area in Terminal 1, the only way up to the Level 7 food court in the data is an escalator. No lift or ramp connects them there, although there are some elsewhere in the terminal. The Midfield Concourse has a single lift in the data, and it only goes up, from Level 1 to Level 6. Someone in a wheelchair can be sent there but not brought back down.

Seen in: C3 (someone who can't use stairs), P2 (wheelchair route to Gate 215).

Before and after security aren't kept apart

At a real airport, once you pass security you can't walk back out to the public halls. The map data doesn't know that: it will route someone from Gate 5 through security into the public Arrivals Hall in 293 m, or send a passenger waiting at the gates to a desk on the public side.

Seen in: P3 (food suggested on the check-in side and in the Arrivals Hall), P7 (walk from the gates to the Lost and Found office).
